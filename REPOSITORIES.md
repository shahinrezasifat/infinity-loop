## 7. Default Installed Source on Boot

When the application is launched for the first time, the following provider is automatically installed, enabled, and selected as the default feed for the Home Screen:

- **Source Name:** `AllMovieLand`
- **Internal Provider ID:** `AllMovieLandProvider` (Composite ID: `cs:AllMovieLand`)
- **Parent Repository:** **Phisher Repo** (`phisherrepo`)
- **Category:** Movies & TV Series
- **Location in Code:** `lib/core/di/injector.dart` (`_seedExistingProviderEcosystems`)
